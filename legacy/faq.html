Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

01 — Booking & Payment

How do I secure my date?+

A non-refundable deposit of 50% of your total services is required to lock in your date. Your date is only held for one week after your contract is sent.

How do I pay?+

Payment is accepted by cash or e-transfer to BlondeBBeauty@gmail.com. No personal cheques. All amounts must be in Canadian dollars.

Can I make changes after booking?+

Small changes like adding or removing a service can be made up to 1 month before your event. No less than half the booked services can be removed after your deposit is paid.

02 — Services & Travel

Do you travel to us?+

Yes! All services are on-location. Travel within Edmonton is a $50 flat fee. Outside Edmonton is $0.60/km each way.

What's included in the trial?+

Your trial is the same price as your wedding day service and is highly recommended so we can perfect your look ahead of the big day.

What if I'm running late?+

If your party is 30 or more minutes late to the appointment, a $95 late fee applies.

03 — Other Questions

Can Krista use photos from my wedding day?+

With your permission, yes! Krista loves featuring stunning looks on social media. You'll be asked when you sign your contract.

What if Krista can't make it?+

In the rare event Krista is unable to attend due to illness or emergency, she will seek a replacement artist and give you as much notice as possible. If no replacement is found, all money paid will be fully refunded.
